Privacy Policy · Last updated October 16, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Ram Prasad General Store handles information when you use its grocery shopping and delivery app or contact the store about an account or order.

Information used to provide the service

  • Account details such as your name, email address, phone number, and password. Passwords are stored by the API as salted hashes.
  • Delivery details you provide, including recipient name and phone, address, delivery instructions, and a map pin or location coordinates when you choose to save them.
  • Basket, saved-item, order, and delivery information needed to prepare, deliver, support, and record your purchases.
  • A profile photo if you choose to upload one, and a device push token if you enable notifications.
  • Theme, language, and notification-list preferences saved on your device.

If you opt in to saved sign-in, the app stores your sign-in details using your device's secure storage. Biometric verification, when available, is performed by the device.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure your account, show the catalog and your basket, calculate delivery eligibility, accept and fulfill orders, provide order updates, answer support requests, and prevent misuse of the service.

Service providers and payments

The app communicates with the store API, which is hosted at ram-prasad-kirana-backend.onrender.com and uses a CockroachDB database. Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to deliver push notifications. Google Maps and related location services are used when you choose map features. These providers process information needed for their services.

The current app checkout supports cash on delivery. The app does not collect or store payment-card numbers. Order and payment-status records are kept to manage and reconcile purchases.

Location

Location access is optional. If you use the address map or device location feature, location information helps set a delivery pin and check whether the address is in the service area. You can enter an address manually instead.

Delivery staff may share their live location while an assigned order is marked as on the way and their delivery panel is open. That recent location is shown only to the customer who placed the matching order; it is no longer shown after the delivery ends or when updates stop. Delivery staff can deny device location permission, but live tracking will then be unavailable.

Retention and account deletion

Account information is kept while your account is active. You can request deletion in the app or by using the account deletion page. After active orders are complete or cancelled, profile and saved account data are removed, and personal details in completed order records are anonymized. Non-identifying order and payment records may be retained where needed for accounting, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile and delivery addresses in the app, manage push permission in your device settings, and change local display preferences in your profile. For a privacy question or data request, contact the store using the details below.
