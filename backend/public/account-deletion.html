Account deletion · Last updated October 16, 2026

From the app

Sign in, open Profile → Help & account → Delete account, and confirm with your current password. For your protection, active orders must be completed or cancelled before the account can be deleted.

If you cannot use the app

Send a deletion request from the email address registered to your account. Include the account phone number so the store can verify the request. Do not send your password or a sign-in code. The store will contact you if additional identity verification is needed.

Email a deletion request Call +977 9824824813

What is removed

Once eligible, the store disables the account, revokes sign-in sessions, and removes profile contact details, profile photo, delivery addresses, saved basket and items, reviews, notifications, and push-notification tokens.

Order records

Order history is not erased where it must be kept for accounting, fulfillment, or dispute handling. Personal delivery details and notes in retained order records are anonymized. Active orders remain available until they are completed or cancelled.
